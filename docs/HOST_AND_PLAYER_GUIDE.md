# The 1% Club — Host & Player Guide

> Replace `<GAME-URL>` below with the live site address before sharing this page.

---

## 1 · Host login

Accounts are created by the **admin** (from the *Host accounts* panel on their dashboard). There is no public sign-up — the admin shares your **email + password** with you directly.

1. Open `<GAME-URL>/host`
2. Sign in with the email and password the admin gave you.
3. You land on the **Host dashboard** — create a room by picking a question set (A / B / C) and clicking **Create room**. Each room gets a 6-character lobby code.

---

## 2 · How players join — two routes

### Route A — Through the website

1. Player opens `<GAME-URL>` and clicks through the intro to **Tonight's Stage → Start the game**.
2. On the registration screen they **enter their name** and select **Multiplayer**.
3. They type the **lobby code** (shared by the host) and hit **Join lobby**.
4. They wait in the lobby until the host starts the quiz.

### Route B — Through the shared play link

1. On the host dashboard (or inside the room page), click **Play link** on the lobby card — it copies `<GAME-URL>/play/CODE`.
2. Share that link with players (WhatsApp, email, anything).
3. Players who open it see the **teaser video → instructions → name screen**, enter their name, tap **get ready**, and land in the lobby.
4. When everyone is in, the host presses **Start** on the room page and can **track every player live** — who has answered, who passed, who's been eliminated, round by round.

---

## 3 · Elimination → playing as a viewer

- A **wrong answer or running out the clock eliminates** the player from the running.
- Eliminated players immediately get a choice: **Continue playing as viewer** or **Just watch**.
- A viewer-player keeps seeing and answering every question in real time, **but their answers are not scored** — they can't win, can't be eliminated again, and don't affect anyone else's game.
- Anyone who opens the play link **after the quiz has started** gets the same offer: join as a viewer and play along unscored.

---

## 4 · The lifeline (skip a question)

- Every player gets **one lifeline per game**.
- It **unlocks at the 50% question** and stays available until used.
- A **"Lifeline · Skip this question"** button appears under the question — tap it once to arm, **tap again to confirm** (so a stray touch can't waste it).
- Skipping **passes the round**: the player survives without answering, but **earns no point** for that question.
- One use only — once spent, it's gone for the rest of the game.
